# SolWhisper v0.7.0

Meetings get a lot lighter on disk, the processing view stops showing you stale content, and voiceprint backfill finally does what 0.6 promised.

## What's new

- 🗜 **Meeting recordings are ~30× smaller** — new recordings are stored as mono AAC (`audio.m4a`) instead of float32 WAV. Same transcription quality for speech, a fraction of the space.
- ♻️ **One-time disk reclaim** — on first launch, your existing WAV recordings are re-encoded to AAC and the originals removed. It's safe: each `.m4a` is verified against the source duration *before* the WAV is deleted, and anything that fails keeps its WAV untouched. Runs in the background; no action needed.
- 🎛 **Focused processing view** — while a meeting runs through Transcribe → Clean → Diarize → Summarize, the detail view now shows just the progress strip and essentials instead of stale transcript/summary content, then refreshes in place the moment it finishes (no more navigating away and back to see the result).
- 👥 **Voice backfill that actually works** — name-only profiles now reliably graduate to stored voiceprints at launch. The 0.6 backfill silently no-op'd on common setups (pure name-stub profiles, a main-actor deadlock behind the permissions prompt); all three causes are fixed.

## Compatibility & known limitations

- **macOS 14+** required.
- **Notarization** — signed but **not yet notarized**. macOS may warn on first launch; right-click → Open (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway) the first time. Auto-update via Sparkle may reset permission grants (Microphone / Speech / Accessibility) until a notarized build ships.

## Upgrade

- **Sparkle auto-update** — installed users are prompted automatically once the appcast updates.
- **Fresh install** — download the DMG from the GitHub release page.
- **Settings & history preserved** — model configs, skill packs, voice profiles, and meeting history carry forward. Recordings are recompressed in place; nothing to re-import.
