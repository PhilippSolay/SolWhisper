## New

**Drag-and-drop audio import.** Drop audio files anywhere on the Transcripts window — or use the new "Upload or drop file" button — to transcribe them. Drop several at once and they queue up, running one after another. Progress shows inline in the window (Transcribe → Clean → Diarize → Summarize → Send) with an end-of-run summary, and imported files now get the same full pipeline as recorded meetings, following your Settings → Meetings toggles.

**Kiros task extraction.** Meetings can extract action items and send them to Kiros, alongside the existing Hermes, Obsidian, and custom-webhook integrations.

## Fixed & hardened

- Data-integrity fixes in the recording pipeline; crash and correctness hardening.
- Security: Keychain handling, header auth, webhook escaping, and MCP token gating.
- OpenRouter key resolution across accounts; settings and paste-field polish.
- Surfaced silent first-run failures; accessibility, contrast, and copy fixes.
